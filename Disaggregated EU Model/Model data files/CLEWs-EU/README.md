# CLEWs-EU model
This folder contains the latest version of the dissagregated CLEWs-EU model, with EU member state detail. Specifically, the following four scenarios are provided:
-	**Baseline scenario (REF)** - this scenario reflects the planned policy framework regarding fuel pricing and taxation; it excludes any strict emissions and renewable energy targets, while the projections are primarily driven by cost optimisation. The baseline scenario also includes the expansion of ETS to other sectors of the economy (i.e., road transport, buildings, light industry).
-	**National NZ scenario (NNZ)** – the greenhouse gas emission targets implemented at the EU level for each EU member state are implemented in this scenario. The 2030 targets are separately enforced for each country, while a net-zero GHG emissions limit by 2050 is implemented for each member state.
-	**Regional NZ scenario (RNZ)** – in order to investigate potential variation in burden sharing, this scenario examines the possibility of achieving the climate neutrality target at the regional level. As such, GHG emissions limits are enforced at the EU level, instead of at the member state level. All remaining assumptions remain constant as in the National NZ scenario. 
-	**Constrained bioenergy scenario (BIO)** – This scenario is identical to the National NZ scenario but the potential increase in the use of biomass for energy is constrained, taking into account limitations across the land-energy-water nexus and potential import limitations. Since in the baseline scenario the use of bioenergy increases substantially (as indicated in section 3), this scenario examines alternative fuel use for decarbonisation.
